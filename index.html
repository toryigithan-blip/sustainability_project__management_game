World Water Week 2026 · Workshop Game

Water Project

Design a water stewardship project to deliver 500 ML/year of water benefit using two resources: a 1,000,000-credit budget and 1,000 hectares of available area. Create one or more workstreams and combine activities based on your strategy. You do not need to use all available resources — focus on building the most impactful solution.

Multiple strategies can reach the target. Build the project that fits your vision.
1 · Create a pillarSet its hectares and define your focus. Add as many activities as your strategy requires.
2 · Build your activitiesCombine Interventions, Measurement & Traceability, Training, and Farmer Extension Services in any combination.
3 · Keep designingReach 500 ML/year, then spend your remaining resources to strengthen additional benefits.
4 · RevealSubmit to see co-benefits, their drivers & trade-offs.
